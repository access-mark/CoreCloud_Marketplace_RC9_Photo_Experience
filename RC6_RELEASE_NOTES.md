# RC6 — Cinematic editorial visual build

## Implemented
- Distinct photographic hero treatments for homepage, business, laptop shop, lifecycle, employee programmes, contact and cart.
- Alternating dark and light editorial sections, larger typography, responsive imagery, hover depth, scroll reveal, and reduced-motion compliance.
- Catalogue uses multiple illustrative photographs rather than identical vector illustrations.
- Preserved RC5 quotation workflow, FormSubmit integration, legacy commerce backend and legal pages.

## Important limits
- Editorial images are loaded from Unsplash CDN at runtime. They are illustrative, not photographs of confirmed inventory or licensed vendor product listings. Network restrictions can prevent them loading; fallback gradients remain. For launch, replace with supplier-approved locally hosted images and check usage rights.
- Live payment, Sage, inventory, customer account, fulfilment and FormSubmit delivery are not verified.
- Browser viewport and end-to-end checks still required; do not mark production ready.
